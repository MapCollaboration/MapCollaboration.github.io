The MAP Collaboration conducts multi-dimensional analyses of partonic distributions through cutting-edge techniques and data-driven approaches.

Key objects of study include:

  • Transverse Momentum Distributions (TMDs)
  • Generalized Parton Distributions (GPDs)
  • Fragmentation Functions (FFs)
